# Physical Book Standards and Specifications

## International Standards

Physical books follow various international standards to ensure consistency and compatibility across different markets and systems.

### ISBN System

The International Standard Book Number (ISBN) is a unique identifier for books and book-like products. Each edition and variation of a book receives a unique ISBN, which is essential for distribution and sales tracking.

### Book Sizes and Formats

Common physical book sizes include:

- **Digest**: 5.5" × 8.5"
- **Mass Market Paperback**: 4.25" × 7"
- **Trade Paperback**: 5.5" × 8.5" or larger
- **Hardcover**: Various sizes, often matching trade paperback dimensions

## Quality Specifications

Physical books must meet specific quality standards for different markets:

- **Library Quality**: Requires durable binding and acid-free paper
- **Educational**: Must meet specific formatting requirements for curriculum use
- **Commercial**: Balances cost and quality for general market

## Paper and Printing Specifications

### Paper Types

- **Text Paper**: Used for interior pages, measured in basis weight
- **Cover Stock**: Heavier paper for book covers, available in various finishes

### Printing Specifications

- **Resolution**: Typically 300 DPI for optimal print quality
- **Color Space**: CMYK for full-color printing
- **Bleeds**: Extra area beyond trim size to prevent white edges

## Accessibility Standards

Modern physical books increasingly consider accessibility, including:

- **Large Print**: Minimum 14-point type for readability
- **High Contrast**: Ensuring text is clearly distinguishable from background
- **Layout Considerations**: Adequate spacing and clear organization
